For a \(C^2\) real scalar field on flat spacetime, let
\[
 e = \tfrac12\bigl(u_t^2+c^2|\nabla u|^2\bigr),\qquad
 F = -c^2 u_t\nabla u,\qquad c>0.
\]
The product rule gives
\[
 \partial_t e + \nabla\cdot F = u_t(u_{tt}-c^2\Delta u).
\]
Assume \(u_{tt}-c^2\Delta u=0\) and that the spatial support of \(u\) stays in a
fixed compact set on each compact time interval under consideration.
Integrate the identity over a ball containing that support and differentiate
under the integral, justified by continuity of \(\partial_t e\) on the compact
space-time region. The boundary flux vanishes since the field is identically
zero near the sphere. Thus \(\int_{\mathbb R^3}e(t,x)\,dx\) is constant there.
