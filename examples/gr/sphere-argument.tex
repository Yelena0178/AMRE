Use \(g_{\theta\theta}=1,\ g_{\phi\phi}=\sin^2\theta,\ g_{\theta\phi}=0\)
on \(0<\theta<\pi,\ 0<\phi<2\pi\). The nonzero connection coefficients
are \(\Gamma^\theta_{\phi\phi}=-\sin\theta\cos\theta\) and
\(\Gamma^\phi_{\theta\phi}=\Gamma^\phi_{\phi\theta}=\cot\theta\).
For \(R^a{}_{bcd}=\partial_c\Gamma^a_{db}-\partial_d\Gamma^a_{cb}
+\Gamma^a_{ce}\Gamma^e_{db}-\Gamma^a_{de}\Gamma^e_{cb}\), contraction gives
\(R_{\theta\theta}=1,\ R_{\phi\phi}=\sin^2\theta,\ R_{\theta\phi}=0\).
Thus \(R=g^{ab}R_{ab}=2\) on the specified chart.
