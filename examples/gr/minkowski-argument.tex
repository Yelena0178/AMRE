In Cartesian inertial coordinates on \(\mathbb R^4\), let
\(g=\mathrm{diag}(-1,1,1,1)\). All \(\partial_a g_{bc}\) vanish.
The Levi-Civita formula then gives \(\Gamma^a_{bc}=0\).
Every derivative and product in the coordinate Riemann formula is zero,
so the Riemann, Ricci and Einstein tensors and the scalar curvature vanish.
